Leading a Small Team

When You Have a Team, and You’re Still the One Holding It Together

You hired good people, and you’re still the one explaining context, checking status and catching what slipped. In SuccessOBM, work is assigned by role, with an owner, a status and a next step, and customer history stays with the customer record. Your team and your OBM work from the same records, and access is role-based with per-feature controls.

Where People Fit

More capacity for the work only people can do

Relationships, empathy, creativity, negotiation, leadership, physical work and high-stakes judgment are where people make the difference, and they’re often what customers value most about a small business. SuccessOBM is built to take on more of the coordination, preparation and follow-up around that work, and to keep business knowledge in shared records rather than with any one person.

Business Fit

Service and product businesses

Service Businesses

Service work runs in sequences: inquiry, quote, approval, job, invoice, follow-up. In SuccessOBM, the engagement stays connected to one customer record. Customers approve quotes online, invoices carry Stripe payment links, follow-ups can be scheduled on a cadence you set, and your OBM can help draft customer updates for your review.

  • Quote, delivery and invoicing in different places
  • Client updates that depend on memory
  • Delivery staff missing context from the sale

Product Businesses

Your OBM can answer supported questions about customers, orders, products and open invoices. Purchase orders can be sent to vendors, invoices carry Stripe payment links, and staff can start a QuickBooks synchronization.

Today, WooCommerce order import is manual and read-only, and supplier ordering is not available.
