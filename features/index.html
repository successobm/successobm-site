Online Business Manager

What your online business manager helps you run

SuccessOBM brings customers, communication, orders, money, marketing, and operational work into one system. Your online business manager can answer questions using connected business information, prepare bounded work, and request approval before consequential actions.

Send email and SMS, manage customers, create quotes and invoices, prepare marketing content, assign work, and track status — from one place. Available features vary by workspace.

Tasks & Reminders

Every action starts as a task

Email, SMS & Forms

Communicate directly from your workflow

Send emails, SMS messages, and forms without leaving the system. Communication is tied to tasks and customers so you always have context and history.

CRM & Customer Records

Keep your customers connected to the work

Store customer information and connect it to tasks, communication, and history. Every interaction is tied to the customer record so you can see what has happened and what comes next.

Marketing Content Workspace

Create and manage content from one place

Manage social posts, blogs, newsletters, and announcements inside SuccessOBM. Draft and review content, then publish to supported destinations — WordPress blogs, and Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn with approval controls — or send newsletters and announcements to your subscribers.

Scheduling & Follow-Up

Keep work moving without relying on memory

Schedule supported sends — such as announcements, emails, and blog posts — and set reminders so follow-up stays visible on the task instead of living in memory.

FAQ

Questions about SuccessOBM features

Current capability areas include:

  • Work: tasks and work management, with owners, statuses, and reminders.
  • Customers: customer records, forms, and subscriber management.
  • Communication: email sending, with inbound replies handled as task messages; SMS with consent controls.
  • Money and orders: quotes with public quote approval, invoices with Stripe payment links, staff-triggered refunds, purchase orders sent to vendors, and QuickBooks synchronization initiated by staff.
  • Marketing: newsletters and announcements, WordPress blog drafting and publishing, and Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn publishing with approval controls.
  • Ask your OBM: questions about supported business information — such as customers, orders, open invoices, products, and open work — answered from connected records.

Not every capability is enabled for every workspace. We’ll walk through what applies to your business during guided onboarding.

Contacts in SuccessOBM aren't a separate database — they're the records that every other platform activity connects to. A contact can be a lead in your sales pipeline, a subscriber on your marketing list, a client with task history and invoices, or all of the above. When you send an email, create a task, or generate a quote, it ties back to the contact record. That means everyone on your team is working from the same information, not pulling from separate tools.

Sends you schedule — such as announcements, emails, or blog posts — go out at the time you set. For many owner-led businesses, it can. Quotes and invoices are created inside SuccessOBM and tied to the customer record and task. Whether it fully replaces your current tool depends on your billing needs — we’ll review that with you in a demo.
