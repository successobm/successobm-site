Ask your OBM

Ask about supported business information, such as customers, orders, open invoices, products and open work, and get answers from your connected records. Open tasks, overdue work and open invoices are also visible in one place.

Answers are only as current and complete as the connected records they’re drawn from.

Available todaySMS includes consent controls

Customers, forms, email and SMS

Keep customer records, forms and subscriber lists in one place. Send email from tasks, with inbound replies handled as task messages. Send SMS with consent controls. When you send an email, create a task or generate a quote, it ties back to the customer record.

Available todaySome steps staff-initiated

Quotes, invoices and orders

Create quotes that customers can approve through a public link. Send invoices with Stripe payment links. Staff can issue refunds, send purchase orders to vendors, and start a QuickBooks synchronization. Quotes and invoices are tied to the customer record and the task.

Available todayApproval (social publishing)

Marketing content

Plan and manage newsletters, announcements, blog posts and social posts. Your OBM can help draft content for your review. Publish to supported destinations (WordPress, plus Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn with approval controls), or send newsletters and announcements to your subscribers.

Available today

Tasks, reminders and scheduling

Turn inquiries, follow-ups, jobs and internal work into tasks with an owner, a status and a next step. Set reminders. Schedule supported sends, such as announcements, emails and blog posts, to go out at the time you set.

Available today

Roles, permissions and team access

Assign work to team members and control access by role. Owners, admins and staff each see the work relevant to them, with per-feature controls.

Available today in supported flows

Approvals

Your OBM can prepare bounded work, such as a draft message or a proposed send. For supported flows, it requests your approval before consequential actions are carried out. Approval-gated execution exists only for those bounded flows, and your OBM does not have unrestricted authority to act on your behalf. Sends you schedule yourself go out at the time you set.

FAQ

Questions about SuccessOBM features

Current capability areas include:

  • Work: tasks and work management, with owners, statuses, and reminders.
  • Customers: customer records, forms, and subscriber management.
  • Communication: email sending, with inbound replies handled as task messages; SMS with consent controls.
  • Money and orders: quotes with public quote approval, invoices with Stripe payment links, staff-triggered refunds, purchase orders sent to vendors, and QuickBooks synchronization initiated by staff.
  • Marketing: newsletters and announcements, WordPress blog drafting and publishing, and Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn publishing with approval controls.
  • Ask your OBM: questions about supported business information — such as customers, orders, open invoices, products, and open work — answered from connected records.

Not every capability is enabled for every workspace. We’ll walk through what applies to your business during guided onboarding.

Contacts in SuccessOBM aren't a separate database — they're the records that tasks, messages, quotes and invoices connect to. A contact can be a lead in your sales pipeline, a subscriber on your marketing list, a client with task history and invoices, or all of the above. When you send an email, create a task, or generate a quote, it ties back to the contact record. That means everyone on your team is working from the same information, not pulling from separate tools.
